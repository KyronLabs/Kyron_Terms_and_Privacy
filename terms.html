Coins & gifts

Test version

Test coins: they cannot be bought, and they have no cash value.

The app says this every time you open the gift sheet. It is the most important sentence in this section.

What coins are

Coins are a feature of Kyron that you use to send gifts to posts you like. They are not money, not a currency, not a deposit and not your property. They are a limited, personal, revocable licence to use that feature, and nothing else.

Where they come from

Right now coins come only from a free weekly top-up. Every Monday at 00:00 UTC your coins are topped up to 100. It is a top-up, not an addition: unused coins do not pile up beyond 100. You cannot buy coins, and nobody — including us — can sell them to you.

Sending a gift

A gift costs the coins shown on it in the gift sheet. When you press Send, the coins are spent and the gift is delivered. A gift cannot be taken back, cancelled or refunded, so check before you send. If sending fails and you press Send again for the same gift, it counts as the same gift: you are never charged twice for it.

Who you can gift

You can gift other people's posts. You cannot gift your own posts, posts that have been deleted, or accounts you have blocked or that have blocked you.

What a gift does — and does not do

The post's author is told who sent what, and the gift count shows on the post. Gifts do not move a post up the feed. Ranking never reads them. Nobody can buy reach on Kyron.

Earned coins

When somebody gifts your post, the coins it cost are recorded as earnings on your account, shown to you only. Earnings cannot be spent, transferred, withdrawn or exchanged for anything, and they have no cash value. We do not promise that they ever will. If we start a programme that pays creators, it will have its own terms, which you will have to accept separately, and it may not count earnings from before it started.

No trading

You may not sell, buy, transfer, lend or trade coins, earnings or accounts that hold them, on Kyron or anywhere else. Gifts are the only way coins move, and only from a sender to the author of a post.

Abuse

We may remove coins, cancel gifts, reverse earnings or stop an account from gifting if we reasonably believe it is being used to cheat — for example, several accounts run by one person gifting each other, automated gifting, or exploiting a bug. Where we do, we will tell you why, and you can appeal as described under Suspension & termination.

If you leave or are removed

If you delete your account, your coins and earnings are deleted with it. Gifts you already sent stay with the people you sent them to. If your account is suspended or banned under these Terms, you may lose your coins and earnings. If a post you gifted is deleted, the gift stays spent.

Changes

This is a test version, and it will change. We may change which gifts exist, their prices or the weekly top-up, or end the test, including resetting everyone's coins and earnings. A gift already sent keeps the price it was sent at. We will tell you in the app before any change that takes away coins or earnings you hold.

Suspension & termination

By you

You can delete your account in Settings → "Delete account". Deletion is irreversible; your public posts disappear within 24h, back-ups within 30 days.

By us

We may suspend or terminate accounts for violations, extended inactivity (>18 months), or legal requests.

What a decision does

  • Removal: a post or reply is taken down. Nothing else changes.
  • Warning: a notice on your account's record. Nothing is restricted.
  • Suspension (1 to 365 days): your account is read-only until it ends. You can still read, and still report, block, mute and hide, so nobody can harass you while you cannot answer.
  • Ban: you can only see why, appeal, contact us and delete your account. Your posts and profile are hidden from everybody else, not deleted: if a ban is overturned, they come back.

We tell you about every decision — a notification, and the details under Settings → Account status, with the rule and what the post said. A new account made with the email address of a banned one starts banned too, and can be appealed like any other ban. Deleting your account does not delete the record of decisions about it, though it does delete what you wrote.

Appeals

Every decision can be appealed once, for 180 days, in the app under Settings → Account status — even from a banned account. Somebody other than the person who made the decision hears it where the team has more than one person; the record shows who decided. If you cannot use the app, email spidroidtechnologiesinc@gmail.com with the subject "Appeal". We review most cases within 72h.
