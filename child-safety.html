Kyron child safety standards

Kyron, the social app made by Spidroid Technologies Inc., does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. These standards apply to every account and everything on Kyron. They say what is not allowed, how to report it, and what we do when we receive a report.

Effective 15 October 2026.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number now. Then report it to us as described below.

What is not allowed

Anywhere on Kyron: in posts, replies, profiles, communities and messages. Here a child is anyone under 18.

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any image, video, audio, text or link that shows or describes the sexual abuse of a child, or that sexualises a child. This includes drawn, animated and AI-generated material.
  • Grooming. Befriending or contacting a child in order to abuse or exploit them sexually, including trying to move the conversation to another app.
  • Sextortion. Pressuring or threatening anyone into sharing sexual images, or threatening to share images of them.
  • Sexualising children. Sexual comments about or to a child, or asking a child for sexual images, conversation or contact.
  • Trafficking and solicitation. Offering, seeking or arranging the sexual exploitation of a child.
  • Sharing it on. Sharing, requesting or linking to any of the above, for any reason, including to criticise it or to "raise awareness".

You must be at least 13 to have a Kyron account. Accounts that belong to a child under 13 are removed.

How to report it in the app

  • A post: tap the ⋯ menu on the post, then Report post.
  • A reply: open the reply's menu, then Report.
  • A person: tap ⋯ on any of their posts and report them. Or, in a conversation with them, open the conversation's details from the top of the chat and choose Report.

Then choose Child safety, and add anything that will help us understand what happened. We do not tell the person you reported who reported them.

Do not screenshot, save, forward or share the material, not even to report it. In many countries, including Ghana, sharing it is a crime whatever the reason. Report it where it is, and we will see it.

Messages

Messages on Kyron are end-to-end encrypted. Only the people in a conversation can read it, and we cannot read it either. That protects everyone's privacy, and it means we rely on you: if somebody sends you anything described above, report them from the conversation and tell us what happened. Block them so they cannot message you again.

Reporting without the app

Email abuse@spidroid.com with the username of the account and a description of what you saw. Do not attach the material itself.

What we do with a report

  • Child safety reports come first. They are reviewed before anything else.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed, and the account that posted or sent it is removed from Kyron.
  • Confirmed child sexual abuse material is reported to the authorities: Ghana's Cyber Security Authority, where Kyron's developer is based, and the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC CyberTipline), which passes reports to law enforcement around the world.
  • We keep what the authorities need to investigate, for as long as the law requires. We respond to valid requests from law enforcement.

You can report to the authorities directly

  • In Ghana: the Cyber Security Authority, by text or call to 292, or by email to report@csa.gov.gh.
  • Anywhere: the NCMEC CyberTipline.

Contact

Questions about these standards, or about how Kyron prevents and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation, go to our child safety contact at abuse@spidroid.com.
