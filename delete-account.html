Kyron

Delete your Kyron account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or by email. Deleting it is permanent: none of it can be recovered afterwards.

In the app

  1. Open Kyron and go to Settings.
  2. At the bottom, under Log out, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tap Delete account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed up with, and say that you want your account deleted. Include your username if you have one. We check the request came from the account's own email address, delete the account, and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

Email privacy@spidroid.com

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, so your email and Google account can no longer reach it
  • Your profile, posts, replies, likes and reposts
  • Your messages, including the ones you sent to other people, and the photos and voice notes in them
  • Your followers, the people you follow, your blocks and mutes, and your settings
  • The photos, videos and voice recordings you uploaded

What happens to what you shared

  • Communities you started are handed to their longest-serving moderator or member, so the people in them keep them. A community with nobody else in it is closed.
  • Messages other people sent you stay with them; your side of those conversations is removed.
  • Posts that quoted yours stay up, without the quote.
  • A picture or banner you set for a community stays with the community.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. We keep an empty record under your old account number, with no email, name or content, so that a phone still signed in to the account cannot bring it back. Copies in our database backups are removed as those backups are replaced.
